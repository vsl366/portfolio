# Credits

## Illustrations

All landscapes (`images/scenes/*.svg`) and the small card scenes are original artwork generated for this site — no third-party images.

## Pac-Man

`games/pacman/` — sprites, mazes, sounds and screens from **Team 2, SSN CSE**'s raylib/C Pac-Man, ported to JavaScript in `js/pacman.js`.
